Arbital / CTO & Founding Engineer

From first customer
to an ISP at scale.

Build the business by building its technology: networks, software, services and operations.

The foundation

I built the initial network, infrastructure and operating software and connected the first customer. As Arbital grew beyond 15,000 active customers, I directed technical work across network and support staff.

An operating platform

The integrated platform covered dozens of use cases, including customer management, provisioning, network policy, monitoring, self-service and staff operations. I wrote the software that translated customer and business needs into network behavior.

Approximately 70–80% fewer overall support requests after subscribers could register replacement devices themselves.

Automation addressed the dominant request category. The measurement window is unspecified.

New services, real infrastructure

I conceived and launched IPTV from scratch as a new revenue stream, starting with multicast and later designing and implementing Nginx-based unicast delivery. I secured LIR status and a public IPv4 /17 allocation, enabling public subscriber addresses without NAT and reducing equipment costs.

I launched Russia's first 100 Mbps plan. Arbital ranked as Russia's fastest ISP on Speedtest.net for several years in the late 2000s. These historical achievements are drawn from my confirmed career record.

Network depth

Architecture and evolution across dozens of external BGP connections, peering, Internet exchanges and carriers, with Linux and FreeBSD systems underneath.
